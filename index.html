The 12,000 Free Books Mission

Help make the hidden player visible.

Colour blindness in football is easy to miss. A mistake can look like a bad pass, poor awareness, nerves or slow decision-making before anyone checks whether the picture was clear.

The goal is to give away 12,000 free books and raise £200,000 to fund free books, awareness marketing, practical resources and colour-blind football education.

12,000 represents the 1 in 12 statistic. £200,000 also carries the 1 in 200 girls statistic — because girls can be colour blind too, and when a girl is affected, the football barrier is just as real.

The numbers football cannot ignore

These figures show why this mission matters. Colour blindness is not a tiny issue. It is easy to miss, and football uses colour everywhere.

Global scale estimate 62.4M Approximately 62.4 million children and adolescents worldwide may be affected.
UK under-18 estimate 634k–636k Approximately 634,000 to 636,000 UK under-18s may be affected.
Male children 1 in 12 Roughly 8% worldwide. This is the number behind the 12,000 free books goal.
Female children 1 in 200 Roughly 0.5% worldwide. Girls can be colour blind too.
💡 Key takeaway: Despite these high numbers, roughly 80% of these children may remain undiagnosed throughout their primary school years. That means many players may be struggling before anyone knows to check colour vision.

These are awareness estimates, not a diagnosis. A standard eyesight check and a colour vision test are not always the same thing, so parents may need to ask specifically about colour vision testing.

Why sponsors are needed

This is not just a book campaign. It is an awareness mission for a hidden football problem.

👀

Colour blindness can be missed

Many children do not know they see differently. A player can pass a normal eyesight chart and still have a colour vision issue if colour vision is not specifically tested.

⚽

Football uses colour everywhere

Kits, bibs, cones, balls, lines, goalkeeper shirts, referee shirts, tactical boards, apps and coaching instructions all rely on colour.

🧠

It can create mental load

A colour-blind player may spend energy checking colours all game. That can reduce focus, slow decisions, create doubt and waste energy.

Questions sponsors may ask

Clear answers help sponsors trust the mission.

Why is the goal 12,000 books?

The number 12 represents the commonly used estimate that around 1 in 12 males have colour vision deficiency. It also connects to the hidden Number 12 player theme in the mission.

Why is the funding goal £200,000?

At an average book cost of £15, 12,000 books equals £180,000. The £200,000 goal gives extra room for delivery, payment fees, awareness marketing, resources, website costs and unexpected costs. It also carries the 1 in 200 girls statistic, reminding people that girls can be colour blind too.

Can I take part if I cannot give much?

Yes. The Take Part Sponsor option is for everyone. Even a small contribution helps move the mission forward and shows that this issue matters.

Can sponsors stay anonymous?

Yes. Sponsors can choose public, anonymous or private when paying. Public sponsors may be listed with their name, business, URL and amount. Anonymous sponsors can be shown as Anonymous Sponsor. Private sponsors are not shown publicly.

Is this only for boys?

No. Colour blindness is more common in boys, but girls can be colour blind too. When a girl is affected, the football barrier is just as real and deserves the same support, dignity and visibility.

What is the practical message?

Do not rely on colour alone when something important must be understood quickly. Football needs clearer cues, better awareness and practical support for players who see colour differently.
